Who can request?

Save the Seerr address and API key to load its Plex users. Enable each Jellyfin viewer and choose that person's account. Their Seerr request limits and approval settings apply. For a Jellyfin-backed Seerr instance, use Automatic.

Enabled viewers can search Seerr's catalog, including titles outside their Jellyfin library restrictions.

In Jellyfin's global search, enter Dune to see library matches and request cards for missing titles. Open a request card and press Favorite. A series card requests season 1. Add s2 or all seasons to choose a different scope. Open the Downloads & Requests tile on the Home Screen (or search downloads) to see active downloads and pending requests.
